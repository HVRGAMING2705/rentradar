🏠 RentRadar — House Rent Predictor

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A ridge regression model trained on 600 synthetic rental listings predicts a house's monthly rent from its area, bedrooms, location tier, furnishing, floor, distance to the nearest metro station and building age. 5-fold CV: R² = 0.909, MAE = ₹6,320 vs a mean-guess baseline MAE of ₹20,291 (mean rent ₹46,367).

How it works: your seven numbers are standardised (subtract the training mean, divide by the standard deviation), multiplied by the model's learned weights, and summed with a bias to give the monthly rent in rupees; predictions are floored at ₹5,000. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try it: a 1,200 sq ft mid-city 2BHK, semi-furnished, 3rd floor, 1.5 km from the metro, 5 years old → about ₹40,324/month. A 2,200 sq ft prime 4BHK, fully furnished, 12th floor, 0.5 km from the metro, 2 years old → about ₹96,340/month. A 600 sq ft suburb 1BHK, unfurnished, 20 years old, 6 km from the metro → the ₹5,000 floor.

Honest note: this model was trained on synthetic listings generated from simple rent logic, so it is a classroom demo — real rents also depend on the exact locality, demand, amenities and negotiation, which seven numbers can't capture.
